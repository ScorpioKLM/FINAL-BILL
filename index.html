Bill Brooks is a lifelong student of biblical prophecy, shaped early on by the study of God's redemption story. After rediscovering a collection of formative prophecy notes, he began drawing them together into a clear, accessible message for modern readers. His book, Prophecy and the Present, explores how God's arc of redemption speaks into today's world with clarity, hope, and purpose.

★★★★★
A Thought-Provoking Journey Through Scripture and History
T. Rollins · Verified Audible Listener

Prophecy and the Present is one of the most intellectually engaging and spiritually enriching audiobooks I have listened to in a long time. Rather than relying on sensationalism or fear, it approaches biblical prophecy with thoughtful research, historical context, and a genuine respect for Scripture. What impressed me most was the balance — complex prophetic themes presented in a way that is both accessible and deeply informative. The narration is warm, engaging, and conversational while maintaining the authority needed for such a meaningful subject. It feels less like someone reading a book and more like sitting across from a knowledgeable friend who genuinely wants to help you better understand God's unfolding plan throughout history. Whether you are new to biblical prophecy or have spent years studying it, there is something here that will challenge your thinking and strengthen your faith. Highly recommended. Five stars!
